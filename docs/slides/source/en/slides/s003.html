Welcome

The drone at a glance

4

brushed 8520 motors, driven straight from the battery through MOSFETs

1

motion sensor (MPU6500). No barometer, no GPS, no compass

250

times per second the control loop reads, decides and sets the motors

One ESP32 chip does everything: the flight control, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth to the phone, and the SBUS radio receiver input.

Three numbers to remember: 4 motors, 1 sensor, 250 loops per second. The single sensor is the most important limit of this drone: everything about height and position later in the course follows from it.
