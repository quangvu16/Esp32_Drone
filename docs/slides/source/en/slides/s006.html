Before you touch the drone: three rules

1

Props off for every test on the desk. A spinning prop cuts skin.

2

Know the stop. CUT MOTORS in the app, or the RC disarm, before you arm.

3

Change one thing at a time, then test it. Two changes at once hide which one broke it.

Welcome · 6 · Source: docs/USER_GUIDE.md §1

Why these rules? Small drones feel like toys, but 8520 motors spin props at tens of thousands of RPM. Rule 3 matters most for learners: when you change the PID gains and a sign at the same time and the drone flips, you cannot tell which change caused it.
