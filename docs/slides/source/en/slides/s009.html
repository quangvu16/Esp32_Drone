How it flies

Thrust against weight decides up or down

Up or down is a tug-of-war between thrust and weight.

Throttle up → climbs. Throttle in the middle → hovers. Throttle down → sinks.

As the battery drains, the same setting gives less thrust — so a fixed setting slowly sinks.

Example

A 40 g drone needs about 40 g of thrust to hover: roughly 10 g from each motor (illustration).

Climbs

thrust > weight

Hovers

thrust = weight

Sinks

thrust < weight

The last sentence explains a behaviour you will meet later: the Hold mode keeps a thrust setting, not a height. With no barometer the drone cannot know its height, so as the battery voltage drops, the same thrust setting lifts less and the drone sinks slowly. Why can’t the firmware simply add more thrust over time? Because it would not know how much — it cannot measure height.
