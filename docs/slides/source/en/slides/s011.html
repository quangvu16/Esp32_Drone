How it flies

Two props spin one way, two spin the other

A spinning prop twists the drone the other way. Mixing directions cancels the twist.

Clockwise: front-right (RF) and rear-left (LR).

Counter-clockwise: rear-right (RR) and front-left (LF).

Each pair sits on a diagonal, so the twists cancel and the drone stays balanced.

Example

Two ceiling fans hanging loose, spinning opposite ways, would cancel each other's twist.

Why does this matter? If all four spun the same way, the drone would spin like a top on take-off. Diagonal pairs cancel the torque. Later, the yaw control uses this on purpose: speed up one pair and the drone turns.
