How it flies

Roll: make one side push harder

To roll right, the left motors speed up and the right motors slow down.

Orange, bigger = faster: LF and LR push harder, the left side rises.

Blue, smaller = slower: RF and RR push less, the right side drops.

Result: the drone tilts right and slides right →

Example

Hover at 50% on all four. Roll right: LF, LR → 55%; RF, RR → 45%. Average still 50%.
