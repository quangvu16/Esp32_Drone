How it flies

Pitch: front against back

To fly forward, push harder at the back.

Rear (RR, LR) faster → the tail rises.

Front (RF, LF) slower → the nose drops.

Thrust now points a little forward, so the drone moves forward ↑

Example

Leaning forward on a bicycle to start rolling: tilt first, then move.

Why does tilting make it move? Thrust always points out of the top of the drone. Tilt the drone and part of the thrust pushes sideways. That is the only way a quadcopter can move horizontally.
