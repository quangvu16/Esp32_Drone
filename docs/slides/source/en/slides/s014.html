Yaw: use the twist on purpose

To turn, use the twist on purpose.

Speed up the CW pair (RF, LR) and slow the CCW pair (RR, LF).

The twists no longer cancel: the body turns the other way (orange arrow).

Total thrust is unchanged, so height is kept.

Example

On an office chair, swing your arms one way: your body turns the other way.

Yaw is the weakest control on a small quadcopter because torque from small props is small. That is why the yaw P gain in the firmware (4.0) is about three times the roll and pitch P gain (1.3).
