The fix is feedback

Example: keeping a car in its lane. You see the drift, compare it to the lane centre, and turn the wheel a little — over and over.

1

Measure what the drone is doing now (the motion sensor).

2

Compare it to what we want (the setpoint from the stick or the app).

3

Correct: change the motors by an amount based on the difference (the error).

4

Repeat — fast enough that errors stay small.

This is closed-loop control. The difference between want and have is called the error. PID, which you meet in section 6, is one recipe for turning the error into a correction.
