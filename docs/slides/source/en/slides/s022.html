The board in one picture

One chip in the middle; everything else plugs into it.

Inputs (left): the motion sensor on SPI, an optional RC receiver on UART, the battery voltage on the ADC.

Outputs (right): PWM to four MOSFET switches, which drive four motors.

Radio (top): the phone talks to the ESP32 over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

Data flows from the sensor into the ESP32, and control flows out of the ESP32 into the MOSFETs and motors. The SBUS receiver is an optional extra input on GPIO4. The battery voltage is also measured, on GPIO36.
