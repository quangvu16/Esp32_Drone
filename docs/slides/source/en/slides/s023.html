ESP32: the brain

Two cores, up to 240 MHz

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth built in

The phone connects directly — no extra radio chip.

Hardware helpers

SPI, UARTs, LEDC PWM and an ADC do the timing work in hardware.

Why an ESP32? One cheap chip gives the processor and both radios. The trade-off: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth stacks also use CPU time and can cause small timing jitter, which is one reason the loop uses a hard 4 ms pace.
