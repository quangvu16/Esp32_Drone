Hardware

SPI: four wires to the sensor

Four wires, one direction each.

SCK: the clock — the ESP32 sets the pace.

MOSI: data out to the sensor. MISO: data back.

CS: LOW means “I am talking to you”.

This firmware runs SPI at 1 MHz, mode 0.

Example

Reading 14 bytes at 1 MHz takes about 0.12 ms — 3% of the 4 ms loop.

SPI is a fast, simple bus: the master (ESP32) drives the clock and the slave (MPU6500) answers. This firmware runs it at 1 MHz in SPI mode 0. Why SPI and not I2C? SPI is faster and has no addressing, so a 14-byte read takes very little of the 4 ms budget.
