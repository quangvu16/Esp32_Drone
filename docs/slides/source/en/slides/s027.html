SBUS: an optional RC receiver

A hobby radio receiver can plug in on GPIO 4 and fly the drone with a real transmitter.

One signal wire

16 channels packed into one 25-byte frame.

Unusual serial

100000 baud, 8 data bits, even parity, 2 stop bits, inverted.

The safety net

SBUS always flies in manual rate mode. Section 8 decodes it.

Example

Serial2.begin(100000, SERIAL_8E2, PIN_SBUS_RX, -1, true); — the final true turns on the inversion.

Hardware · 27 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino sbusInit()

Why is SBUS inverted? It was designed by Futaba with an inverted signal. The ESP32 UART can invert in hardware, so the firmware passes true as the fifth argument of Serial2.begin. No inverter chip is needed.
