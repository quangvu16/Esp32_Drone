AO3416 MOSFET: the motor switch

A GPIO pin cannot power a motor. A MOSFET is an electronic switch that can.

Small signal in

The ESP32 pin drives the gate with 3.3 V and almost no current.

Big current through

The MOSFET lets battery current flow through the motor.

Fast

It can switch on and off 20,000 times a second.

Example

Like a light switch you flip with one finger that controls a big lamp.

Hardware · 28 · Source: Alpha & Omega AO3416 datasheet; Esp32_drone.ino PIN_MOTOR comments

Why this part? It is a small N-channel MOSFET that turns fully on with a low gate voltage, so a 3.3 V ESP32 pin can drive it directly. One MOSFET per motor: Q1 to Q4 on the schematic.
