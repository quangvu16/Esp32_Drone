PWM: fast on-off makes an average

On-off, very fast. The motor feels the average.

Duty cycle: the fraction of each cycle the switch is ON.

20 kHz: one cycle every 50 µs — too fast for the motor to notice.

10 bits: duty is a number from 0 to 1023.

Example

Battery 4.0 V, duty 256 of 1023 (25%) → the motor feels about 1.0 V.

Why 20 kHz? Below about 20 kHz, the motor coils make an audible whine. 20 kHz is at the edge of human hearing, and still slow enough to give 10-bit duty resolution on the ESP32 LEDC timer.
