Hardware

The battery: one LiPo cell (1S)

One LiPo cell (1S): voltage is the fuel gauge.

A full cell is 4.2 V.

Below 3.5 V the firmware warns (VBAT_CELL_WARN).

Below 3.3 V it refuses to arm, and lands itself if it stays there for 3 s (VBAT_CELL_CRIT).

Example

Thrust also drops with voltage — that is why hover height sinks during a flight.

Why care so much about voltage? Lithium cells are damaged if drained too far, and a weak cell sags under load, so the drone can suddenly lose thrust. The firmware refuses to arm below 3.3 V and lands itself when the battery stays critical for 3 seconds.
