If the props are reversed: YAW_DIRECTION

Some builds put CW props on RR and LF instead. Then every yaw correction pushes the wrong way.

Symptom

The drone spins faster and faster as soon as it lifts off.

Fix

Change #define YAW_DIRECTION from 1 to −1.

Why it works

The mixer multiplies the yaw output by YAW_DIRECTION, flipping all four yaw signs.

Hardware · 36 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino #define YAW_DIRECTION, motorMix()

This is a configuration sign, not a bug. The same idea appears for the gyro and accelerometer axes (GYRO_X_SIGN and others): if the sensor is mounted rotated, you flip a sign instead of changing the maths.
