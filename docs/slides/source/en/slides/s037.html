The power path

One battery feeds three paths. Line thickness = current.

Motors take most of the current, straight from the battery.

Logic needs a steady 3.3 V, so it goes through a regulator.

The divider lets the firmware watch the voltage.

The motors take most of the current, straight from the battery. The logic needs a steady 3.3 V, so it goes through a regulator. When the motors pull hard, the battery voltage dips — which is why the voltage reading is filtered.
