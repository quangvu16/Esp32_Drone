What setup() does, in order

1

motorInit() and motorStop() — motors off before anything else.

2

mpuInit() — if WHO_AM_I is wrong, print a wiring hint and stop forever.

3

ADC attenuation for the battery pin, then sbusInit().

4

Open NVS and cfgLoad() — saved PID gains and flight settings.

5

linkInit() (Wi-Fi / Bluetooth), then mpuCalibrateGyro() and a first battery read.

Architecture · 43 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino setup()

Why motors first? At power-on the GPIO pins can float, and a floating MOSFET gate could spin a motor. Driving them to zero duty first is the safe default. Why calibrate the gyro last? The radio start-up takes time; calibrating last means the bias is measured closest to when you fly.
