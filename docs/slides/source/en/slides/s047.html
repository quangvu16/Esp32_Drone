Sensor data joins twice: angles at self-level, gyro rates at the PID.

Example

Stick right → sp_roll_rate +50 °/s → gyro says 0 → error −50 → PID output → right/left motors change → the drone starts to roll.

The sensor joins in two places: the filtered angle feeds the self-level step, and the gyro rate feeds the PID. Follow one example: the pilot pushes roll right, the setpoint becomes a roll rate, the PID sees the drone is not rolling yet, the mixer speeds up one side, and the drone rolls.
