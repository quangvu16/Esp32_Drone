Inputs: who can fly the drone

Phone app (CRTP)

Over Wi-Fi UDP, Bluetooth Classic SPP or BLE. Wins whenever its link is live.

RC receiver (SBUS)

Optional. Used when the phone is silent. Always manual rate mode.

Serial console

USB cable, for bench setup: link, sta, save, load, defaults, status.

Example

Phone connected and sending → phone flies. Phone app closed for 0.5 s → SBUS takes over if a receiver is fitted; if not, failsafe stops the motors.

Why does the phone win? The phone sends richer commands (modes, takeoff, land). Why keep SBUS? It is the safety net: if the phone link drops, a real RC transmitter can still fly. Section 8 covers both protocols, and section 9 explains how the code decides which input is in charge.
