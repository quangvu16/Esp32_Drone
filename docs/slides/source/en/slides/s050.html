Outputs: what the firmware produces

Motor PWM

Four duty values, every 4 ms. The only output that moves the drone.

CRTP replies + logs

Acks for commands, parameter values, and up to 3 log streams to the phone.

Serial telemetry

One human-readable line per second: VBAT, sticks, angles, motors.

Architecture · 50 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino motorWrite(), logUpdate(), telemetry()

Telemetry and logs are how you debug. The serial line is for the bench with a USB cable; the CRTP logs are for flight, read by the phone app. Section 11 shows how to read both.
