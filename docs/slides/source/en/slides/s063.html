Tilt from gravity: the accelerometer angle

The accelerometer finds the tilt from the share of gravity on each axis.

At rest the only force is gravity. Tilted, part of it falls on the flat axis.

asin(ay ÷ total) turns that share back into an angle.

Only true when the drone is not accelerating: a punch or a bump makes it jump.

Example

Tilted 30°: ay = sin 30° × 1 g = 0.5 g → asin(0.5) = 30°.

30°

gravity = 1 g

ay = sin 30° = 0.5 g

az = cos 30°
= 0.87 g

drone tilted 30°

This angle never drifts, because gravity always points down. But it is only true when the drone is not accelerating. During a punch of throttle or a bump, the accelerometer feels extra force and the angle jumps. That is why it cannot be used alone.
