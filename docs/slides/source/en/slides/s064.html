The complementary filter idea

Combine them: mostly trust the gyro, and slowly pull toward gravity.

Each loop: move the angle by what the gyro says (fast, smooth).

Then nudge it 0.4% toward the accelerometer angle (slow, never drifts).

The result keeps the good half of each.

Why combine instead of picking the better one? Neither is better: the gyro is right over a fraction of a second, the accelerometer is right over several seconds. A complementary filter is the simplest way to take the short-term part of one and the long-term part of the other. Bigger flight stacks use a Kalman or Mahony filter for the same job.
