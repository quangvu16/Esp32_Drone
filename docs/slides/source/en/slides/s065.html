The filter in code

angle_pitch_deg += gx * dt;      // 1. predict with gyro
angle_roll_deg  += gy * dt;
angle_pitch_deg = angle_pitch_deg * 0.996f
                + angle_pitch_acc * 0.004f;   // 2. correct
angle_roll_deg  = angle_roll_deg  * 0.996f
                + angle_roll_acc  * 0.004f;

Step 1: move the angle by what the gyro says happened this loop.

Step 2: blend 99.6% of that with 0.4% of the accelerometer angle.

On the very first loop, the angle starts from the accelerometer (gyro_angles_set).

Example

Angle 10.00°, gyro says +0.04° this loop, accel says 9.0° → (10.04 × 0.996) + (9.0 × 0.004) = 10.036°.

That is the whole filter: two lines per axis. It is called complementary because the two weights add to 1, and the gyro covers high frequencies while the accelerometer covers low frequencies.
