Trade-off: a bigger accelerometer weight, such as 0.02, removes drift faster but lets vibration into the angle. A smaller one, such as 0.001, is smoother but drifts for longer after a bump. Roughly one second is a common choice for small drones. If you change LOOP_TIME_US, the weights must change too, or the time constant moves.
