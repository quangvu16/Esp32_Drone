Motion sensor

Heading drifts — and the code says so

Roll and pitch have gravity to correct them. Heading (yaw) has nothing.

How it is made

heading_deg += gz × dt, wrapped to 0…360°.

Why it drifts

No magnetometer: any leftover gyro bias adds up forever.

What it affects

Heading hold in HOLD/AUTO and the frame for navigation.

Example

Leftover bias 0.05 °/s → about 3° of heading drift per minute of flight.

Motion sensor · 67 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino attitudeUpdate() comment

The comment in attitudeUpdate() reads: heading hold is only as good as the gyro bias over a flight — it drifts slowly; that is a real limit, not something to paper over. Navigation works around this by asking the phone, which has a compass, to supply the starting bearing (NAV_CMD_ALIGN).
