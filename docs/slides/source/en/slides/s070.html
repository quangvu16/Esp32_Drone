PID control

PID in one analogy: a shower

P looks at now, I at the past, D at the future.

P only: pushes hard, overshoots, wobbles — and stops a little short.

P + D: D brakes the swing, so it is calm — still a little short.

P + I + D: I removes the last gap, so it settles on the target.

Example

Shower: P turns toward hot, I keeps adding while it is still cold, D eases off when it heats up fast.
