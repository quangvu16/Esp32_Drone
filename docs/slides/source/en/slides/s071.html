PID control

P: push in proportion to the error

P = Kp × error. Double the error, double the push.

Roll and pitch Kp

1.3

Yaw Kp

4.0 (yaw torque is weak)

Too much P

The drone shakes fast (oscillates). Too little: it feels soft and lazy.

Example

The drone rolls at 10 °/s but we want 0 → error 10 → P = 1.3 × 10 = 13 units of motor correction.

P alone usually leaves a small steady error, because once the error is small, the push is small too. That is the job of I. P alone also tends to overshoot, which is the job of D.
