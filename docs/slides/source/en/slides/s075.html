PID control

The sign of the error matters

Here error = measured − wanted (gyro − setpoint). Many textbooks write wanted − measured.

Either works

…as long as the mixer signs match the choice.

The rule

A positive output must push the drone toward a negative rate.

If one sign is wrong

The correction becomes a push, and the drone flips at once.

Example

Test with props off: tilt the drone by hand while armed. The motors on the low side must speed up. If the high side speeds up, a sign is wrong.

Why point this out? Signs are the most common bug in drone firmware, and they hide in four places: the gyro axis signs (GYRO_*_SIGN), the error, the self-level term and the mixer. This firmware had one such bug in the self-level term, which you will see on slide 81.
