Wind-up: why I is limited and reset

Wind-up: I grows while it cannot act.

On uneven ground a small error never goes away, so I keeps growing.

At lift-off that stored I tips the drone hard.

Two fixes: clamp I to ±400, and reset it whenever the throttle is at idle.

Example

Error 2 for 5 s on the ground → I = 0.04 × 2 × 1250 = 100 units stored before lift-off.

Example of the problem: on the ground, the drone sits 3 degrees tilted on uneven grass. The level loop asks for a rate the drone cannot achieve, the error stays, and I keeps growing. Without the reset, the drone would leap sideways the moment it lifts off.
