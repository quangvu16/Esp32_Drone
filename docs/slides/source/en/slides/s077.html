PID control

D on error, and its side effect

Both damp real motion the same way. They differ only when the setpoint jumps.

D on error (this firmware)

D = Kd × change in (gyro − setpoint)

Simple: one formula

A sudden stick move changes the setpoint → a one-loop spike (derivative kick)

D on measurement (alternative)

D = Kd × change in gyro only

No kick from stick moves

Slightly more code; the common choice in big flight stacks

Ask why the kick is small here: the stick setpoint changes smoothly and the output is clamped to ±400.

Derivative kick: if the pilot snaps the stick, the setpoint jumps in one loop, so the error jumps, and D multiplies that jump by 18. The motors get a one-loop spike. In this firmware it is limited because SBUS sticks move smoothly, the phone sends angles that go through the self-level P term first, and the output is clamped. Changing to D on measurement is a good learning exercise.
