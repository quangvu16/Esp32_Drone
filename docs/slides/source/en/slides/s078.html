PID control

Limits that keep control possible

±400

PID output clamp (PID_LIMIT): one axis cannot take all the motor range

1800

THROTTLE_MAX: full stick leaves 200 of headroom for corrections

1100

armed minimum per motor: props never stop in the air

Example: throttle 1800, roll correction +250 → right motors want 2050 → clamped to 2000. Without the 1800 cap they would have no room at all.

PID control · 78 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino #define PID_LIMIT, THROTTLE_MAX; motorMix()

Why leave headroom? If the throttle is at 2000, a motor that needs more for a correction cannot go higher, so control is lost exactly when it is needed. Why a minimum of 1100? A stopped brushed motor takes time to restart, and a prop that stops mid-flight cannot correct anything.
