PID control · 84 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino #define PID_*; PARAM_TOC ranges

Tuning order that works for most small drones: one, set I to zero; two, raise P until fast shaking starts, then back off 30%; three, raise D until the drone stops bouncing after a quick stick flick; four, add I until it holds level without drifting; five, save. Change one value at a time, and test with props off first.
