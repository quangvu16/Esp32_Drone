Mixer and motors

The mixer: four lines

motor_rf = t - pitch + roll - yaw * YAW_DIRECTION;
motor_rr = t + pitch + roll + yaw * YAW_DIRECTION;
motor_lr = t + pitch - roll - yaw * YAW_DIRECTION;
motor_lf = t - pitch - roll + yaw * YAW_DIRECTION;

t = throttle, the same on all four.

Roll: + on the right pair, − on the left pair.

Pitch: + on the rear pair, − on the front pair.

Yaw: + on one diagonal (RR, LF), − on the other (RF, LR).

Example

Each column of signs sums to zero: roll + + − − = 0, pitch − + + − = 0, yaw − + − + = 0.
