Worked example: one loop of mixing.

PID outputs this loop: pitch −20, roll +50, yaw +10. Throttle 1400.

RF = 1400 + 20 + 50 − 10 = 1460; RR = 1400 − 20 + 50 + 10 = 1440.

LR = 1400 − 20 − 50 − 10 = 1320; LF = 1400 + 20 − 50 + 10 = 1380.

Example

Average = (1460 + 1440 + 1320 + 1380) ÷ 4 = 1400 — mixing moves thrust around; it never adds any.
