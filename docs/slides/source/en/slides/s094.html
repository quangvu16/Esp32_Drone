Mixer and motors

Saturation: when a motor hits its limit

If one motor needs more than 2000 or less than 1100, it is clipped. The balance between the four is lost.

What you see

At full throttle the drone feels less stable; it may yaw or wobble.

Why

The clipped motor cannot give its share of the correction.

Built-in help

THROTTLE_MAX 1800 and PID_LIMIT ±400 make clipping less likely.

Example

Throttle 1800, roll +300 → right motors 2100 → clipped to 2000. 100 units of roll correction are lost.

Bigger flight stacks add “air mode” or “mixer scaling”: when a motor would clip, they shift all four down together so the corrections survive. This firmware does not, which is a good extension project once you understand the mixer.
