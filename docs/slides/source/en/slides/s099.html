A real bug: reading the wrong byte

Off by one byte, the failsafe flag was never seen.

Before (bug)

Flags were read from byte 24, the footer

Footer is always 0x00 → flags always look clear

A receiver in failsafe kept the motors running on stale sticks

After (fix)

Flags read from byte 23

Lost or failsafe frames are dropped

The 500 ms timeout then stops the motors; sbus_test.cpp pins it

Radio inputs · 99 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino sbusFrameUsable(); test/sbus_test.cpp

Why is this so dangerous? A receiver that loses the transmitter usually keeps sending frames with the LAST stick positions and the failsafe flag set. If you never look at the flag, the drone flies on with whatever throttle the pilot last had, out of control. The fix also refuses to refresh sbus_last_frame_ms on a failsafe frame, so the normal timeout does the stopping.
