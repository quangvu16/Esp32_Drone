Radio inputs · 101 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino armUpdate(), #define YAW_ARM_THRESHOLD

Why hold for a second? A brief bump of the stick must not arm. Why throttle low? So the motors never jump to a high setting the instant they arm. Arming also runs armChecksOk(), so a battery below 3.3 V refuses to arm.
