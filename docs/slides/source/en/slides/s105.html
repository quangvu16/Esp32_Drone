Sanity checks: NaN or infinity → ignored; |roll| or |pitch| over 180, or |yaw| over 2000 → ignored.

Radio inputs · 105 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino crtpHandleCommander()

crtpHandleCommander() copies these with memcpy, then rejects the packet if any float is NaN or infinite, or the angles are absurd (more than 180 degrees, or a yaw rate over 2000). A bad packet is ignored, so the last good command stays and the failsafe timer keeps running.
