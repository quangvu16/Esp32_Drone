TOC = table of contents: the app downloads the list of names, types and access.

Each read-write entry carries a lo and hi range.

The reply echoes what was actually stored, so the app shows the truth.

Example

The app writes roll_kp = 1000 → stored as 100 (the hi limit) → the echo tells the app 100.

Radio inputs · 107 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino PARAM_TOC, paramStore()

Why validate? Before this fix, a NaN gain or a takeoff_ms of 0 from the app reached the flight code. takeoff_ms is a divisor in the take-off ramp, so 0 meant a divide by zero. Now every write is checked, and values loaded from flash are checked too (paramSanitizeAll).
