AUTO is a small state machine

AUTO is a small state machine: one simple rule per state.

TAKEOFF moves to HOLD by itself when the ramp ends.

HOLD lasts until LAND is tapped or the battery goes critical.

LAND finishes by disarming and going back to MANUAL.

Example

Tap Take off → 2 s climb → hover → tap Land → 2.5 s descent → disarmed.

TAKEOFF moves to HOLD by itself when the ramp ends. HOLD lasts until the app sends LAND, or the battery goes critical. LAND finishes by disarming. Why a state machine? Each state has one simple rule, and the transitions are easy to test and to explain on a screen.
