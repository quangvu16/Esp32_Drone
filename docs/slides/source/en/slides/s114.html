The take-off and landing ramps

Take-off and landing are straight-line ramps.

t runs from 0 to 1 over each phase, so thrust is a straight line.

Thrust (0–65535) becomes 1000–2000: 1000 + thrust ÷ 65535 × 1000.

Landing is purely timed — there is no sensor that sees the ground.

Example

hover_thrust 30000, takeoff_ms 2000 → at 1 s the thrust is 15000 → 1229 µs.

time (s) →

thrust (0–65535)

1 s: 15000 → 1229 µs

take-off 2 s

hover

land 2.5 s
