Heading hold in HOLD and AUTO

Heading hold: always turn the short way.

Heading error → yaw rate: 2 °/s per degree, capped at 200 °/s.

The wrap (±360 when past ±180) picks the short way round.

The hold heading is captured on entering HOLD or at take-off.

Example

Hold 10°, heading 350° → herr = −340 → +360 = +20 → yaw rate 40 °/s.

now 350°

hold 10°

short way: +20°
→ 40 °/s

long way: −340°
(never taken)

Why wrap? Headings are on a circle. Without the wrap, a drone at 350 degrees asked to hold 10 degrees would turn 340 degrees the long way round instead of 20 degrees the short way. The hold heading is captured when you enter HOLD or take off, so the drone keeps facing where it was facing.
