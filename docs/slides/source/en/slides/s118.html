Modes and safety · 118 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino loop() failsafe block

Why does MANUAL need stick packets specifically? In MANUAL the stick stream IS the setpoint; without it the last thrust would stay latched. In HOLD or AUTO the drone flies itself and the app sends other packets (mode, log requests), so any traffic proves the phone is still there. Before the fix, only stick packets counted, so a drone hovering in AUTO cut its motors after 500 ms.
