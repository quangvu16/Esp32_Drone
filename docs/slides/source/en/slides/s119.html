Modes and safety

A real bug: time measured backwards

One millisecond, read in the wrong order, could cut the motors.

Before (bug)

Ages compared as unsigned numbers

A packet handled this loop can be 1 ms newer than now

now − last = −1 → wraps to ~49 days → failsafe → motors cut

After (fix)

(int32_t)(now − last) < FAILSAFE_MS

−1 ms is simply “very fresh”

Signed comparison still works across the 49-day wrap

Modes and safety · 119 · Source: Esp32_drone.ino loop() comment

The subtle part: now is read at the top of loop(), and the receivers run after that. A packet that arrives during sbusPoll() gets a timestamp of millis(), which can be one millisecond later than now. As unsigned 32-bit numbers, now − last is then 4,294,967,295 — about 49.7 days — and the failsafe fires for one loop, cutting the motors mid-flight. Casting the difference to signed fixes it.
