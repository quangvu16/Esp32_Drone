Modes and safety

Bad numbers never reach the motors

Every value from outside is checked before it is used.

Commander packets

NaN, infinity or absurd angles → ignored.

Parameter writes

NaN refused; others clamped into the TOC range.

Hover thrust

clamped to 8000 – 55000 from SET_HOVER, TAKEOFF and flash.

Flash at boot

a non-finite saved gain restores all defaults.

Why so many checks? Data from a phone, a radio link or flash memory can be corrupted, or simply wrong because of an app bug. The flight loop trusts its inputs completely, so the checking has to happen at the edges, where data comes in. This is called validating at the boundary.
