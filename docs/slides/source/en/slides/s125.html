Modes and safety

CUT MOTORS, end to end

Tap

2 taps on Fly, 1 tap on Mission and Tune

→

App sends

port 7: 0x70 0x05 (EMERGENCY)

→

Firmware

flightEmergency(): disarm, motorStop, pidReset

→

Mode

back to MANUAL, AUTO idle

→

Reply

0x70 0x05 0x00 → app shows stopped

EMERGENCY has no conditions. It is the one command that always works.

Example

Drone drifting toward a wall in HOLD → double-tap CUT MOTORS → props stop within one 4 ms loop of the packet arriving.

Why two taps on Fly? The Fly screen is where your thumbs are, so an accidental single tap is likely; the second tap confirms. On Mission and Tune you are not flying with your thumbs, so one tap is enough. EMERGENCY is honoured in every mode and state: no check can refuse it.
