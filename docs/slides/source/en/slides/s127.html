Modes and safety

Safety in layers

No single check is perfect, so they are stacked.

Arming: battery ≥ 3.3 V, thrust seen low, ARM only from idle.

Inputs: checksums, NaN and range checks, SBUS flags.

In the air: 500 ms failsafe, signed time, auto-land.

Limits: tilt ±25°, PID ±400, throttle 1800, motor floor 1100.

CUT MOTORS: honoured in every state.

No single check is perfect, so the firmware stacks them, like slices of Swiss cheese: a hole in one layer is covered by the next. When you add a feature, ask which layer it belongs to and whether it opens a hole in any other.
