Navigation

Dead reckoning: guessing position from motion

The phone knows where it is (GPS). The drone only knows how it has been accelerating.

Phone

Works out the target as metres north and east of the launch point, and sends it.

Drone

Adds up its own acceleration to guess how far it has moved since launch.

Controller

Tilts toward the difference between the target and the guess.

Example

Walking across a dark room by counting steps: fine for a few metres, badly off after a long walk.
