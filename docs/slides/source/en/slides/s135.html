The launch frame and NAV_CMD_ALIGN

The launch frame: +x where the nose pointed, +y to its left.

No compass on the drone: the phone sends the nose's bearing at launch — ALIGN.

Targets arrive as metres north and east; two lines turn them into the launch frame:

x = n·cos a + e·sin a, y = n·sin a − e·cos a.

Example

a = 90°, target 10 m north: x = 10·0 + 0 = 0, y = 10·1 − 0 = 10 → 10 m to the left. ✓
