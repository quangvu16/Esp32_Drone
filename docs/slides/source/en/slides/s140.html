Testing · 140 · Source: Esp32_drone/test/

Why test on a PC? No drone, no props, no batteries, and a result in about a second. These tests do not try to cover everything. They cover the lines that were once wrong and that are easy to get wrong again: signs, frames and protocol edge cases. Every one keeps the old, buggy version as a negative control.
