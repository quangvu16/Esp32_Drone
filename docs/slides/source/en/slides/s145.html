Open the serial monitor at 115200 baud. Once a second the firmware also prints a telemetry line. link, sta and save are refused while armed. Example line: “disarm | VBAT:3.92V | RC r:1500 p:1500 t:1000 y:1500 | ang p:+0.4 r:-0.2 | mot RF:1000 RR:1000 LR:1000 LF:1000”.
