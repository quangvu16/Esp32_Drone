Build and flash

1

Install the esp32 by Espressif board package in Arduino IDE 2.x (or arduino-cli).

2

Open Esp32_drone/Esp32_drone.ino. Board: ESP32 Dev Module.

3

Partition scheme: Huge APP — required, the radios need about 1.7 MB.

4

Upload, then open the serial monitor at 115200.

5

Keep the drone flat and still during the gyro calibration message.

Testing · 146 · Source: docs/INSTALL.md §1

Why Huge APP? Wi-Fi, Bluetooth Classic and BLE together compile to about 1.7 MB, which does not fit the default 1.25 MB partition. Huge APP gives 3 MB and drops over-the-air updates, which a drone does not need. If the boot stops at a WHO_AM_I message, check the SPI wiring first.
