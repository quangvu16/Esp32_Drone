Your learning path: five exercises

1

Read loop() and write down which numbered step changes each global variable.

2

Bench: props off, arm, tilt the drone by hand and watch which motors speed up.

3

Log: chart pid.roll_p, roll_i and roll_d in the app while hovering. Which term does the work?

4

Test: break the self-level sign on purpose and watch level_test fail. Then fix it.

5

Extend: change D to act on the measurement, or add mixer scaling — with a test first.

Do them in order. Each one uses what the previous one taught. Exercise 2 is the most important safety habit: every time you touch a sign anywhere, repeat it before flying.
