Deep questions to keep asking

Why is every sign bug in this firmware a flip, not a wobble?

Hint: positive feedback grows without limit.

Why does each missing sensor create exactly one limit in a mode?

Hint: map barometer, compass, GPS, rangefinder to HOLD, heading, FOLLOW, landing.

Why are most safety checks at the edges of the code?

Hint: where does untrusted data come in?

Why keep the buggy version inside every test?

Hint: how do you know a test can fail?
